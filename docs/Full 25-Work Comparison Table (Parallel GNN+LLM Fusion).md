# Literature Survey: Parallel GNN + LLM Dual-Encoder Fusion Architectures

**Scope.** Prior work in which (a) a graph encoder (GNN/GAT/GCN/graph transformer) captures structural/topological signal, (b) a language model / transformer captures semantic/textual signal, and (c) the two representations are fused for a downstream classification task. Emphasis on whether the two encoders run **in parallel** (independent, fused only at the end) and whether fusion is **truly attention-based with learned per-sample weights** — the two properties that define the target design (Phase 1 / Step 3 of "LLM-Enhanced Graph-Based Intrusion Detection").

**Grounding.** Every architectural and numerical claim below was extracted from a page fetched in this session. Where a source did not state a detail (very common for fusion equations in journal HTML that does not render LaTeX), this is marked explicitly as *not stated in fetched source* rather than guessed.

## 2. Synthesis

### (a) Which fusion mechanisms dominate

Across the 25 works above, four families recur, in roughly descending order of frequency:

1. **Concatenation + MLP/FC head (late fusion).** The default. GMLM concatenates the projected RGCN embedding with the PLM `[CLS]` vector into a 2·d_PLM vector and passes it through a two-layer fusion MLP, and its own comparison table lists cross-attention, gating and learned modality weights as *not used* ([GMLM](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.05763v3)). VulnSense concatenates BERT, BiLSTM and GCN branch outputs into dense layers ([VulnSense](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2309.08474)). GCN-2-Former global-average-pools each encoder and concatenates before an FC+Softmax head ([GCN-2-Former](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-18401-3)).
2. **Cross-attention (one- or two-way).** CAST fuses coGN node embeddings with text tokens by cross-attention to recover global crystal information the graph loses ([CAST](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.06836v1)); DCVD runs *bidirectional* cross-attention with explicit Q/K/V assignments in both directions ([DCVD](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.11015v1)); BiGTex uses Q from the GNN, K/V from the PLM ([BiGTex](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.12474v2)); BotCF fuses semantic/property/community features by cross-attention ([BotCF](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/237643/1/T-NSM2025.pdf)); the molecular-science survey documents Q-Former cross-modal projectors as the standard in GIT-Mol and MolCA ([Segura-Alabart & Serratosa](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/42/7/btag387/8708323)).
3. **Gating.** CPS-IDS uses a "Gated Fusion Mechanism" over its semantic and statistical branches ([CPS-IDS](https://www.techscience.com/cmc/v87n3/66942)); the phishing BERT+HGT model uses "gated attention" cross-modal fusion with 8 heads in a 512-d fusion space ([Amedu et al.](https://ijisrt.com/assets/upload/files/IJISRT26MAY1421.pdf)); the AI-agent threat-detection work uses "gated multi-view fusion" that "adaptively combines both representations", lifting social-engineering AUC while preserving structural-attack generalization ([Structural Representations for Cross-Attack Generalization](https://huggingface.co/papers/2601.01723)). The canonical primitive is the Gated Multimodal Unit, which "learns to decide how modalities influence the activation of the unit using multiplicative gates" ([Arevalo et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.01992)).
4. **Scalar weighting / prediction interpolation.** BertGCN interpolates the GCN prediction with an auxiliary BERT prediction using a single interpolation weight ([BertGCN](https://aclanthology.org/2021.findings-acl.126.pdf)); GETE uses a meta-learned ensemble with "dynamic weighting based on the characteristics of the input data" ([GETE](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-31653-3)).

A fifth family — **contrastive alignment without fusion** (MoMu, MoleculeSTM) — aligns the two embedding spaces but never combines them for a classifier; DCVD shows the productive hybrid, using contrastive alignment as a *pre-fusion* regularizer (`L_align`, Eq. 5) and cross-attention as the actual fusion.

### (b) Does anything already do the exact target design? — **Yes, one work comes very close, and it matters for the novelty claim**

**The single closest prior work is the Dual-Stream Graph-Augmented Transformer of Moorthy et al., *Scientific Reports* 15:25436 (2025)** ([fetched source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12259997/)). It runs **BERT in parallel with a GAT + Graph Transformer stream**, then fuses them with a **trainable feedforward attention-scoring function that emits a scalar weight per modality per input**, described in the paper's own words as adaptively balancing the streams — relying more on textual indicators for deceptive language and more on graph features for suspicious propagation patterns. That is, mechanism-for-mechanism, the target design's "per-sample attention weights determining how much to rely on structural vs semantic evidence." It reports ~99% accuracy and 0.99 AUC on FakeNewsNet (PolitiFact + GossipCop).

Two qualifications keep the target's contribution defensible:
- **Domain.** That work is fake-news detection over social propagation graphs. Nothing equivalent exists for NetFlow/IoT intrusion detection: in the IDS literature fetched here, GNN+LLM combinations are either **sequential** (LLM explains the GNN's output in [XG-NID](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.16021v1); LLM filters/reweights the graph *before* the GNN in [Zhan et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.20806)), or use a **non-linguistic** second tower ([GCN-2-Former](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-18401-3)), or have **no graph tower at all** ([CPS-IDS](https://www.techscience.com/cmc/v87n3/66942), [BSTFNet](https://www.techscience.com/cmc/v78n3/55933/html)). The only IDS preprint that combines LLM semantic encoding with GNN embeddings via a transformer fusion detector ([FedLLM IDS](https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-7738954/v1)) states no fusion mechanism, no modality weights and no attention-based interpretability.
- **Rigour of the interpretability claim.** Neither the *Scientific Reports* paper's Eqs. 15–17 nor GETE's meta-learner is documented well enough in the fetched sources to establish that per-sample modality weights are validated as *interpretable* (e.g. attention mass shown to be structurally dominant for DDoS and semantically dominant for injection/XSS). The molecular GNN+LLM survey records **no model-specific interpretability claim** for any of the seven fusion architectures it reviews. Per-attack-class validation of learned fusion weights is therefore an open, claimable contribution.

The honest novelty framing: *parallel GAT + LM with learned per-sample attention fusion is not new as a mechanism (it exists in misinformation detection, and closely related gated variants exist in phishing and AI-agent threat detection), but it is unattested for NetFlow-based network intrusion detection, and per-attack-class validation of the learned structural-vs-semantic weights appears unattested anywhere.*

### (c) Design patterns worth borrowing for the fusion classifier

- **Copy DCVD's fusion stack almost verbatim** — it is the best-documented parallel structural(GAT)+semantic(LLM) fusion available ([DCVD](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.11015v1)): (i) an InfoNCE **alignment loss** `L_align = -(1/B)Σ log[exp(sim(g_i,c_i)/τ) / Σ_j exp(sim(g_i,c_j)/τ)]` on positive (same-flow) `h_v`/`s_v` pairs to shrink the cross-modal distribution gap *before* fusion; (ii) **bidirectional cross-attention**, `H_s = softmax(Q_sK_tᵀ/√d′)V_t` and `H_t = softmax(Q_tK_sᵀ/√d′)V_s`; (iii) pool-broadcast-concatenate-project `H = σ(W_m[H_t ‖ H̄_s])` with d′=128. The alignment loss is cheap insurance against one branch dominating simply because its embeddings have larger norms.
- **For the interpretable per-sample weights, use the modality-scoring form, not full cross-attention.** Compute `a_h = score(h_v)`, `a_s = score(s_v)` with a shared small MLP (tanh or ReLU single hidden layer), softmax-normalize over the two modalities, and fuse as `z = α_h·h_v ⊕ α_s·s_v` (or apply the weights to the two halves of the concatenated vector). This is the mechanism the *Scientific Reports* dual-stream model describes ([source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12259997/)) and it yields exactly two reportable numbers per flow — ideal for the DDoS-vs-injection interpretability story. Note that source does not confirm softmax normalization, so state your choice explicitly rather than citing it.
- **Add a gate as an ablation arm.** A GMU-style multiplicative gate `g = σ(W_g[h_v ‖ s_v])`, `z = g ⊙ tanh(W_h h_v) + (1-g) ⊙ tanh(W_s s_v)`, gives *dimension-wise* input-dependent mixing rather than one scalar per modality ([GMU](https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.01992)); gated fusion is what CPS-IDS and the phishing BERT+HGT model use in security settings, and gated multi-view fusion is what recovers linguistic-attack performance in AI-agent threat detection ([source](https://huggingface.co/papers/2601.01723)). Scalar-attention weights are more interpretable; dimension-wise gates usually score higher. Report both.
- **Include a self-attention-over-modality-tokens variant.** RAGFormer stacks the two branches' embeddings into a short sequence and applies `Y_seq = SelfAttention(X_seq)` with a residual `X_fused = X_gaga + Y_seq` before the MLP head ([RAGFormer](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.17472v1)). With two tokens (`h_v`, `s_v`) this is a one-line implementation and generalizes cleanly if a third channel (e.g. raw flow statistics) is added later.
- **Ablation ladder, grounded in real baselines.** (1) `h_v` only; (2) `s_v` only; (3) concat + MLP — literally GMLM's fusion, which explicitly forgoes attention and gating ([GMLM](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.05763v3)); (4) fixed-λ prediction interpolation — BertGCN's mechanism, the key contrast since its weight is a global hyperparameter, not per-sample ([BertGCN](https://aclanthology.org/2021.findings-acl.126.pdf)); (5) gated fusion; (6) the proposed per-sample attention fusion. Steps (3) and (4) let you demonstrate that the *per-sample* nature of the weights, not merely having two towers, is what buys performance and interpretability.
- **Terminology and positioning.** "Structural encoder / semantic encoder" is established vocabulary — DET uses precisely those names, and explicitly argues *against* concatenating separate encoders' outputs in favour of interleaving them ([DET](https://aclanthology.org/2024.lrec-main.419.pdf)); GL-Fusion goes further and folds message passing into every transformer layer ([GL-Fusion](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.06849v1)). Cite both to pre-empt a reviewer asking why late parallel fusion is preferable: your answers are per-sample interpretability and encoder modularity.
- **Two extra structural ideas.** BotCF adds an explicit **macro community channel** (DANMF community membership) alongside a shallow GCN precisely because a 2-hop GCN cannot see macro structure ([BotCF](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/237643/1/T-NSM2025.pdf)) — directly relevant if `h_v` is claimed to capture community behaviour. GMLM's **dynamic active-node selection**, encoding text only for selected nodes and zero-filling the rest, is a practical way to keep the LM branch affordable on large flow graphs ([GMLM](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.05763v3)).
- **Motivation framing.** CAST's argument — that graph conversion loses global information which text descriptions restore ([CAST](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.06836v1)) — is the cleanest published statement of why a parallel text branch is not redundant with the graph branch, and transfers directly to serialized NetFlow triples supplying attack semantics (port 22, `initiated_scan`, high frequency) that a topology-only GAT cannot express.

### Gaps and caveats in this survey

- Fusion **equations** could not be confirmed for several relevant works because publisher HTML did not render LaTeX in the fetched pages: the *Scientific Reports* dual-stream Eqs. 15–17, GETE's meta-learner, CPS-IDS's gate, the phishing model's gated attention, GATiT's "interactive attention-based fusion", and BotCF's cross-attention Q/K/V. These are flagged inline; do not cite them as confirmed formulas.
- MoMu and MoleculeSTM were only available as abstract pages here, which state contrastive learning but no encoder or fusion details; treat them as alignment precedents only.
