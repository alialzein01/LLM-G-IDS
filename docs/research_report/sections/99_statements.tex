\section*{Statement on the use of AI assistance}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Statement on the use of AI assistance}

AI assistance was used for drafting and editing prose in this report, for writing and
refactoring pipeline and analysis code, and for building the scripts that audit the
manuscript's numbers against the committed result artifacts. Every experiment was designed and
run by the author. Every number reported here comes from a committed artifact produced by
those runs, and no figure, table or quantitative claim was generated from a model's
recollection. The interpretation of the results, the decisions about what to measure, what to
retract and what to report as negative, and the final text are the author's own.
